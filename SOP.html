Amazon Data Entry SOP & Knowledge Hub

Standard Operating Procedure for Amazon Product Content Creation & Template Population
1. Core Research, Client Data & Verification Rules +
  • 100% Client Data Utilization (Crucial First Step): Carefully extract and use ALL basic information provided in the client’s data file. Do not miss or skip any data. Once your entry task is completed, perform a final cross-verification scan against the source file to ensure zero data omission.
  • 100% Strict Facts Only: Every statement, attribute, feature, or specification must be a verified product fact. Never guess, estimate, or assume any detail (material, dimensions, weight, color, compatibility, durability).
  • No Assumption Rule: Do not add details simply because they are common in the product category (e.g., do not write Dishwasher Safe, BPA Free, or Scratch Resistant unless explicitly verified).
  • Source Hierarchy (Priority Order):
    1. Client-provided data & high-res product images.
    2. Official Brand / Manufacturer website or documentation.
    3. Authorized distributors & reliable retailers.
    4. Amazon & established e-commerce platforms.
  • Exact Product Match: Verify identifiers (Brand, Model, Barcode/EAN/UPC, ASIN, SKU, Variant, Pack Size) before extracting details.
  • Different Pack Size Rule: Core facts (ingredients, material) apply across pack sizes, but total pack weight, volume, piece count, and bundle contents must be independently verified.
  • Missing Information: If an attribute cannot be verified after deep research, strictly leave it BLANK.
2. Template Attribute Classification Rules +

Every product template contains 4 types of attributes. Follow these specific execution rules:

  • Required Attributes: Must be filled 100% with verified facts.
  • Conditional Required Attributes: Must be filled 100% based on product conditions/category triggers.
  • Recommended Attributes: Research and fill as many as possible using verified facts to achieve maximum listing quality.
  • Optional Attributes: Fill only details found during standard research. Do not spend extra time specifically digging for optional attributes.
3. Mandatory Top 10 Attributes Rule +
  • Mandatory Rule: Check the provided Top 10 Attributes File for the specific product type. The 10 attributes listed for that category MUST be filled, regardless of whether they appear as Required, Recommended, or Optional in the template.
  • Exception Rule for Top 10 Only: If a Top 10 attribute fact cannot be verified after deep research, a logical similar-product value may be added only if genuinely applicable.
  • Validation Requirement: Any unverified Top 10 value MUST be bolded and highlighted in the attributes table and flagged in the Validation Notes section for manual review.
4. Product Title Creation & Marketplace Limits +
  • Standard Title Format: Brand + Model Name + Key Feature + Product Type + Size/Weight/Volume + Color
  • Compatibility Format: Brand + Key Feature + Product Type + for + Compatible Devices + , Color
  • Marketplace Limits:
    • ZA (South Africa Marketplace): UP TO 75 CHARACTERS MAXIMUM. Title must be extremely concise while retaining essential product facts.
    • Other Marketplaces (AE, SA, EG, TR, etc.): Keep character limits as usual (standard limit up to 150–200 characters).
  • Rules: Use verified facts only—no promotional fluff (Best, Premium, Top, Guaranteed). Do not repeat words. Include a comma before size or color where applicable.
5. Bullet Points Creation & Structural Order +
  • Quantity & Length: Create 3 to 5 bullets. Keep each bullet concise, factual, readable, and scannable (20–150 characters).
  • Customer-Oriented, NOT Marketing Text: Focus on factual relevance (what it is, what it does, what it includes). Do NOT use marketing sales fluff ("makes your life easy", "unbeatable quality").
  • Small Spare Parts / Simple Components Exception: For minor items like simple spare parts, basic auto parts, small electronic components, or simple accessories where limited detail exists, creating even just 1 bullet point is acceptable. Do not add artificial filler text.
  • 5-Point Structural Order:
    1. BP1: Main Product Benefit / Key Feature → Why should I buy it?
    2. BP2: Core Feature / Functionality → What does it do?
    3. BP3: Key Specifications → What are its important specs? (Size, material, power, compatibility)
    4. BP4: Design / Convenience / Additional Features → What additional features make it better/easier?
    5. BP5: Usage / Application / What's Included → How/where do I use it or what do I get?
6. Product Description Rules +
  • Character Limit: Minimum 200 to Maximum 2,000 characters.
  • 3-Paragraph Structure:
    • Paragraph 1 (Introduction): Product type, main purpose, and primary function.
    • Paragraph 2 (Features): Verified materials, dimensions, capacity, design details, and included components.
    • Paragraph 3 (Usage Context): Practical, factual explanation of how the customer uses the product.
  • Rules: Write naturally without keyword stuffing or repeating the title word-for-word. Do not copy-paste bullet points. Avoid unverified functional claims (e.g., do not claim Dishwasher Safe unless verified).
7. Pre-Submission Quality Checklist +
  • [ ] Have you fully utilized ALL basic information provided in the client file without missing anything?
  • [ ] Have you done a final scan against the client/source data to cross-check for completeness?
  • [ ] Are all information and claims based 100% on verified product facts?
  • [ ] Are all Required and Conditional Required attributes filled 100%?
  • [ ] Are all attributes from the Top 10 Attributes File filled (even if optional in template)?
  • [ ] Are unverified Top 10 values bolded, highlighted, and listed under Validation Notes?
  • [ ] Were Recommended attributes filled to maximum capacity without using guesswork?
  • [ ] Is the Product Title within the target marketplace character limit? (<= 75 characters for ZA; standard limit for AE, SA, EG, TR)
  • [ ] Are Bullet Points short, easy to read, fact-based, customer-oriented, and free of marketing fluff?
  • [ ] Are all promotional/marketing words (Best, Premium, Superior, Perfect) removed?
  • [ ] Is the Product Description original, factual, well-structured, and between 200–2,000 characters?
