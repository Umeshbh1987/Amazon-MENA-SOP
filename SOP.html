Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Reference Guide

This manual establishes the mandatory quality and compliance guidelines for generating Amazon Global and MENA catalog listing assets (Product Titles, Descriptions, Bullet Points, and Generic Backend Keywords). Strict adherence to character thresholds, factual precision, and regional marketplace policies is required across all listings.

1. Product Title 2. Product Description 3. Bullet Points (2-Tier System) 4. Generic Keywords 5. Prohibitions & Policies
🏷️ 1. Formatted Product Title
Global: ≤ 200 Chars | ZA: ≤ 75 Chars
📌 Length & Regional Caps
  • Global Marketplaces (AE, SA, EG, US): Maximum 200 characters (including spaces).
  • South Africa (ZA): Strictly capped at ≤ 75 characters total length.
  • Title Case: Capitalize the first letter of each major word (exclude standard articles, conjunctions, and prepositions).
🚫 Characters & Repetition
  • Prohibited Characters: Strictly avoid ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ — (permitted only if legally certified as part of the official brand name).
  • Repetition Limit: Do not repeat the same content word more than 2 times across the title.
  • Numerals Requirement: Always express numbers as numeric digits (e.g., 8, not "eight").
🎯 Construction Formula
  • Standard Formula: [Brand] + [Model / Sub-brand] + [Key Feature] + [Product Type] + [Size / Weight / Color / Pack].
  • Zero Promotional Claims: Prohibit subjective terms such as "Best Seller", "Free Shipping", "100% Quality Guarantee", or "Top Rated".
Compliant Example: DEWALT TSTAK Deep Tool Box with Heavy Duty Wheels and Long Handle 23 L Capacity
📝 2. Product Description
200 – 2000 Characters
📄 Structural Integrity
  • Single Cohesive Paragraph: Must be drafted as exactly ONE continuous narrative paragraph.
  • No Bullet Points or Breaks: Bulleted lists, numbering, line breaks, or detached snippets are strictly prohibited.
  • Length Threshold: Must contain between 200 and 2000 characters.
🎯 Opening Sentence Protocol
  • Title-Aligned Lead: The opening sentence must begin with the starting phrasing of the generated title up to the Product Type component.
  • Brand & Model Placement: Brand, Model Number, and Model Name are allowed only at the beginning and must not be repeated in middle or closing sentences.
🔍 Content & Tone
  • Quantitative Emphasis: Prioritize measurable, quantitative data over subjective adjectives (e.g., state weighs 2.5 kg instead of lightweight).
  • Comprehensive Details: Seamlessly integrate value propositions, applications, primary benefits, technical specifications, materials, wattage, and capacities.
  • Exclusions: Strictly exclude commercial, warranty, promotional, and logistical details (such as customer support, shipping, or returns).
⚡ 3. Bullet Points (2-Tier Classification System)
< 250 Chars Per Bullet | No Terminal Period
Tier 1: Standard Products (Comprehensive Technical Data Available)
3 to 5 Bullets Required
  • Quantity: Generate 3 to 5 distinct, impactful bullet points.
  • Character Limit: Each individual bullet point must be strictly under 250 characters.
  • Header Construction: Begin each bullet point with a capitalized summary benefit header followed by a hyphen (-).
  • No Terminal Punctuation: Do NOT place a period (.) or any terminal punctuation at the end of any bullet point.
  • Diverse Scope: Each bullet must address a distinct subject (e.g., Primary Feature, Core Benefit, Practical Application, Compatibility, Material Composition, Unique Selling Proposition, or Maintenance Guideline).
High Load Capacity - Carries up to 220 lbs of TSTAK modules, providing a heavy-duty solution for transporting large tool collections
Tier 2: Low-Information & Small Component Parts
1 Bullet Sufficient (Max 2 with Verified Data)
  • Target Items: Fasteners, nuts, bolts, washers, minor electronic parts, simple manual tools, and small automotive spare parts with limited technical scope.
  • Standard Rule: Exactly 1 comprehensive, high-impact bullet point is sufficient to state core dimensions, material grade, and functional compatibility.
  • Conditional Exception (2 Bullets): A second bullet point is permissible ONLY IF authentic, verified technical documentation yields substantial separate data worthy of inclusion. Never add filler bullets.
  • No Terminal Punctuation: Do not end the bullet with a period (.).
Corrosion Resistant Build - Manufactured from grade 304 stainless steel providing high tensile strength and rust prevention for outdoor fastener assemblies
🔑 4. Generic Backend Keywords
8 – 12 Phrases | Semicolon (;) Separated
📋 Structure & Volume
  • Phrase Count: Provide exactly 8 to 12 relevant keyword phrases.
  • Phrase Length: Each phrase must consist of 2 to 4 words.
  • Mandatory Delimiter: Separate phrases strictly with a semicolon (;).
🎯 Search Intent & Ordering
  • Intent Balance: Maintain a balanced combination of broad customer search queries and specific attribute terms.
  • Logical Ordering: Sequence keyword phrases from broader category classifications to narrower specific terms.
  • Exclusions: Never include brand names, ASIN identifiers, competitor trademarks, or internal punctuation within phrases.
heavy duty tool box; mobile storage organizer; modular tool chest; rolling workshop carrier; rolling tool case; professional hardware chest; portable organizer container; stackable toolbox cart
⚠️ 5. Policy Enforcement & Prohibited Content
Mandatory Policy
🚫 Prohibited Commercial Phrasing
  • "Best Seller" / "Top Rated" / "#1 Rated"
  • "100% Quality Guaranteed" / "Satisfaction Guarantee"
  • "Free Shipping" / "Fast Delivery" / "Prime Eligible"
  • "Discounted Price" / "Lowest Price Available"
🚫 Restricted Symbols & Elements
  • Forbidden Characters: ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ —
  • Trademark Marks: ® ™ © (unless legally verified in brand title)
  • Emojis, non-standard graphical symbols, or excessive ALL CAPS typography
⚠️ Catalog Health Advisory: Violations of character limits or inclusion of prohibited claims can lead to Amazon search suppression, buy-box ineligibility, or catalog listing restrictions. Always enforce strict factual verification prior to publishing.
