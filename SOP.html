Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) Reference Guide

Yeh manual Amazon Global & MENA catalog content generation (Title, Description, Bullets, Generic Keywords) ke updated rules define karta hai. Listing creation ke time 100% facts verification aur regional limits mandatory hain.

1. Product Title 2. Product Description 3. Bullet Points (2-Tier System) 4. Generic Keywords 5. Prohibitions & Policies
🏷️ 1. Formatted Product Title
Global: ≤ 200 Chars | ZA: ≤ 75 Chars
📌 Length & Regional Limits
  • Standard Marketplaces (AE, SA, EG, US): Maximum 200 characters (including spaces).
  • South Africa (ZA): Strictly ≤ 75 characters total length.
  • Title Case: Har word ka first letter capital hona chahiye (except standard articles/conjunctions).
🚫 Symbols & Repetition
  • Prohibited Characters: ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ — strictly banned hain (unless brand name ka verified legal part ho).
  • No Word Repetition: Same word 2 times se zyada repeat nahi ho sakta (articles/prepositions excluded).
  • Numerals Only: Numbers ko hamesha digits me likhein (e.g. 8, not "eight").
🎯 Structure Template
  • Title Formula: [Brand] + [Model/Sub-brand] + [Key Feature] + [Product Type] + [Size/Weight/Color/Pack].
  • Zero Fluff: "Best Seller", "Free Shipping", "100% Guarantee" jaise promotional words strictly prohibited hain.
Compliant Example: DEWALT TSTAK Deep Tool Box with Heavy Duty Wheels and Long Handle 23 L Capacity
📝 2. Product Description
1200 – 2000 Characters
📄 Paragraph Format
  • Single Cohesive Paragraph: Poora description exactly ONE continuous narrative paragraph hona chahiye.
  • No Lists / Breaks: Description ke andar koi bullet point, list ya line break nahi hona chahiye.
  • Length Limit: Strictly 1200 se 2000 characters ke beech hona zaroori hai.
🎯 First Sentence Rule
  • Title Alignment: Pehla sentence generated title ke starting words se shuru hoga, specifically Product Type field tak.
  • Brand/Model Control: Brand Name, Model Number aur Model Name sirf starting me aayenge; unhe beech ya aakhir me repeat na karein.
🔍 Content & Tone
  • Quantitative Specs: Actual measurements ko subjective words se upar rakhein (e.g. weighs 2.5 kg instead of lightweight).
  • Cover Details: Value proposition, applications/use cases, benefits, materials, wattage, capacity ko natural flow me weave karein.
  • Zero Commercials: Shipping, warranty, branding ya seller logistics include na karein.
⚡ 3. Bullet Points (2-Tier Classification System)
< 250 Chars Per Bullet | No Terminal Period
Tier 1: Standard Products (Sufficient Data Available)
3 to 5 Bullets Required
  • Bullet Count: 3 se 5 distinct aur impactful bullets generate karein.
  • Character Limit: Har bullet point strictly 250 characters se kam hona chahiye.
  • Formatting Structure: Bullet hamesha capitalized summary benefit header se shuru hoga, jiske baad hyphen (-) aayega.
  • No Terminal Period: Bullet ke end me period (.) ya koi bhi terminal punctuation NAHI lagana hai.
  • Distinct Topics: Top Feature, Key Benefit, Compatibility, Material/Build, Unique Selling Point ya Maintenance Tips cover karein.
High Load Capacity - Carries up to 220 lbs of TSTAK modules, providing a heavy-duty solution for transporting large tool collections
Tier 2: Low-Info & Small Component Parts
1 Bullet Sufficient (Max 2 on Verified Data)
  • Applicable Items: Fasteners, nuts, bolts, washers, small electronic components, small tools, automotive spare parts jinki minimal specs hoti hain.
  • Standard Rule: Aise products ke liye EXACTLY 1 single high-impact bullet hi sufficient hai jo core size, material grade aur compatibility bataye.
  • Exception (2 Bullets): Agar verified website se solid, technical aur consider karne layak alag information milti hai, tabhi 2nd bullet banayein; zabardasti fluff na jodein.
  • No Terminal Period: End me period (.) nahi lagana hai.
Corrosion Resistant Build - Manufactured from grade 304 stainless steel providing high tensile strength and rust prevention for outdoor fastener assemblies
🔑 4. Generic Backend Keywords
8 – 12 Phrases | Semicolon (;) Separated
📋 Structure & Count
  • Quantity: Exactly 8 to 12 keyword phrases generate karein.
  • Phrase Length: Har phrase 2 to 4 words ka hona chahiye.
  • Delimiter: Phrases ke beech hamesha semicolon (;) lagayein.
🎯 Search Intent & Order
  • Mix: Broad categories aur specific attribute search terms ka balance rakhein.
  • Arrangement: Keywords ko broader-to-narrower category sequence me arrange karein.
  • Prohibitions: Brand names, ASINs, ya competitor brand names keyword phrases me allow nahi hain.
heavy duty tool box; mobile storage organizer; modular tool chest; rolling workshop carrier; rolling tool case; professional hardware chest; portable organizer container; stackable toolbox cart
⚠️ 5. General Compliance & Prohibited Terms
Strict Policy
🚫 Banned Promotional Words
  • Best Seller / Top Rated
  • 100% Quality Guaranteed
  • Free Shipping / Fast Delivery
  • Lowest Price / Huge Discount
🚫 Banned Symbols & Characters
  • Special Characters: ! $ ? _ { } ^ ¬ ¦ —
  • Intellectual Property Marks: ® ™ © (unless verified in brand name)
  • Emojis, decorative icons, ya full capital words (e.g. "NEW", "BUY NOW")
⚠️ In rules ka violation hone par Amazon catalog team listing ko search-suppress ya ASIN freeze kar sakti hai. Hamesha factual aur measurable data hi include karein.
